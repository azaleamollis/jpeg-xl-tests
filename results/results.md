## 1. Provenance

Blockiness ratio = at the original resolution, the mean absolute luma difference between neighbouring pixels across the 8x8 block boundary phase with the highest value, divided by the mean of the other seven phases (average of horizontal and vertical). 1.00 means no 8-pixel periodicity. 'clean' means **no sign of JPEG history** in this test, not proof of none; resampling or cropping after a JPEG stage can hide the grid. The verdict rule is my own heuristic (not a standard): `suspicious` if ratio >= 1.05 and it exceeds both control periods (7 and 9 px) by more than 0.02; `unknown` if there is almost no gradient signal. Per-phase values and controls are in `results/provenance_details.csv`.

**Scorer:** SSIMULACRA 2 via `ssimulacra2.exe` from the libjxl v0.12.0 release (the JPEG XL project's reference implementation, ships with cjxl/djxl, takes PNG input). Chosen because it is the original implementation and needed no building here. It has no version flag.

**Fixed encoder settings (same for every image):**

- JPEG: Pillow/libjpeg-turbo, `quality=Q`, Huffman optimization, baseline, 4:2:0, no metadata (no cjpeg/ImageMagick; see deviations).
- WebP: `cwebp -q Q -m 4 -mt -metadata none`; decoded with `dwebp -nodither`.
- AVIF: `avifenc -q Q -s 6 -j all -y 444 -d 8`; decoded with `avifdec`.
- JXL: `cjxl -q Q -e 7 --quiet`; decoded with `djxl`.
- Lossless: `cwebp -lossless -z 6 -m 4 -mt -metadata none`; `avifenc --lossless -s 6 -j all`; `cjxl -d 0 -e 7`; `oxipng -o 4 --strip safe`. Variants only when needed: `cwebp -exact`.
- Chroma subsampling therefore differs between formats (JPEG and WebP 4:2:0, AVIF 4:4:4, JXL none). I did not change it per format.

**Matching rule:** per image and format, integer quality 1 to 100, binary search starting at 50, at most 8 iterations, stop at the first trial whose SSIMULACRA 2 score vs the working PNG is within 70 +/- 1. If none is within the band, the cell says NOT REACHED and shows the closest trial; nothing is adjusted. The first in-band trial is taken, not the closest to 70, so scores range from 69.02 to 70.91 across reached cells. Sizes are the encoded file sizes, including container headers.

**Working copies:** Lanczos-3 (my own implementation with the same geometry as Pillow's resampler, because Pillow cannot resize 16-bit RGB), applied to the original-bit-depth data and rounded back to that depth; then one conversion to 8-bit sRGB. Long edge 2000 px; images at or below 2000 px keep their size (no upscaling): `coaf-logo` and `vscode-jxl-rs`. Resizing was done on encoded (gamma) values, not linear light. Working PNGs are untagged 8-bit, written with Pillow (compress level 6). SHA-256, dimensions and factors are in `results/working_copies.csv`.
