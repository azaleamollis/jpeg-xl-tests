## 9. Method and limits

- **Not a benchmark.** Six images, one machine, one metric. No averages, rankings or winners; no encode or decode times were measured or are reported in this round (the separate decode-speed test is described in the README). A handful of images cannot support general conclusions.
- **Inputs:** the 8-bit sRGB working PNGs from round 1 (`work/*.png`, long edge <= 2000 px), unchanged. `dsoc-laser.tif` stays excluded. `sources/` was not modified.
- **Score:** libjxl 0.12.0 `ssimulacra2.exe`, decoded result vs working PNG. coaf-logo (real alpha) is composited on gray 128 for both before scoring, as in round 1. Decoded dimensions were checked for every trial.
- **Search (round 1 method):** integer quality, binary search over 1..100 starting at 50, at most 8 iterations, stop at the first trial within the target +/- 1 (84 to 86, or 69 to 71). If none lands in the band the cell is marked NOT REACHED and the trial whose score is closest to the target is reported; nothing is adjusted. All encoders use their own native 0..100 integer quality scale. Because the score is not always smooth in the setting, a NOT REACHED cell can mean that no integer setting lands in the band, as in the cases described in section 8.
- **Two JPEG encoders, exact settings:**
  - `JPEG (libjpeg-turbo, baseline)` (unchanged from round 1): Pillow 12.3.0 with libjpeg-turbo 3.1.4.1, `Image.save(dst, format="JPEG", quality=Q, optimize=True, subsampling=2 (4:2:0), progressive=False)`, no metadata.
  - `JPEG (MozJPEG 4.0.3, progressive)`: `cjpeg-static.exe -quality Q -outfile out.jpg in.ppm` (mozjpeg version 4.0.3, build 20210225; official prebuilt `mozjpeg-v4.0.3-win-x64.zip`, https://github.com/mozilla/mozjpeg/releases/tag/v4.0.3, SHA-256 of the zip c8db69b2bbf9cff05447e60454b55317f719d9793b9100597bfcd4682836f7ee). Only `-quality` was given; the remaining flags are MozJPEG's defaults (progressive, optimised Huffman tables, trellis quantisation, DC scan optimisation 1, chroma subsampling chosen by the encoder subsampling varies with quality, see section 11 (4:2:0 was seen at q75; 4:2:2 and 4:4:4 at higher qualities)). The PPM input was written by the scripts from the working PNG because this cjpeg does not read PNG. MozJPEG output is decoded with Pillow (same decode path as the libjpeg-turbo column).
  - **Which JPEG to compare against:** I recommend comparing against `JPEG (MozJPEG 4.0.3, progressive)`, the second encoder that was actually used. It is *an* optimised JPEG encoder, not "the best JPEG": `cjpegli` / `djpegli` were not available and were not tested, MozJPEG 4.0.3 is not the newest tag (newer tags, v4.1.1 to v4.1.5, have no prebuilt Windows binaries on the official release page and were not built from source or tested), and the two columns differ in more than one setting (progressive vs baseline, trellis vs none), so differences between them are not attributable to a single feature.
- **Recompression test (part A):** the JPEGs were made by me from the working PNGs (not taken from cameras or CDNs). `cjxl <in>.jpg <out>.jxl -e 7 --quiet` (default = lossless JPEG recompression), `djxl <out>.jxl <restored>.jpg --quiet`, SHA-256 compared. Savings are measured on these 16 files only.
- **Reuse:** target 70 for libjpeg-turbo, WebP, AVIF and JXL is round 1's result, not a re-run. Round-1 decoded PNGs in `work/lossy/` were used for the crops after checking that each encoded file's size equals the recorded bytes (no mismatch).
- **Crops:** 500x350 at 100%, centre box `left=(W-500)//2, top=(H-350)//2` fixed before looking at any image; summer-tanager (2000x1227) left=750 top=438, vscode-jxl-rs (1920x1080) left=710 top=365. Not resized. No crop was viewed or displayed.
- **Metric limits:** SSIMULACRA 2 is a single, perceptual-model-based score; matching it does not mean the images look the same to every viewer, and a +/-1 band means sizes are only approximately matched. Looking at the images (not done here) is a separate step.
